03 / Operational logic

Drip schedule, triggers and exit rules

Timing is relative to each customer's entry. Event-based transitions override scheduled drips. The schedule is a proposed pilot configuration, not a universal sequence to send to everyone.

Priority rules

  1. Investment complete: stop all acquisition drips and meetup solicitation.
  2. In active investment process: pause general marketing, send only appropriate service help.
  3. Signed up for meetup: suppress other consideration invitations; send event logistics.
  4. Style Finder complete: suppress initial quiz invitations and basic awareness.
  5. Basket viewed: prioritise educational basket details over generic promotion.
  6. Inactive: cap frequency and move to cooling status.

04 / Campaign engagement concept

Smartfolios Style Finder · interactive prototype

Experience the five-question discovery flow as a customer. This prototype demonstrates interest-led content; it does not calculate a formal risk score or recommend a specific basket.

5 questions / ~60–90 secQuestion 1 / 5

Design principles

Begin with interests

Ask about objectives and investment tastes before more sensitive questions about risk comfort.

Show progress and explain why

One question per screen; short language; no account opening or lead capture to start.

Use existing customer context carefully

Avoid unnecessary repeated questions where authenticated and permissioned data are available.

Do not manufacture a match score

Show an interest profile and links to product information, not “92% suitable for Basket X”.

If the customer needs capital protection, has a short horizon or cannot afford equity losses, do not lead with an investment CTA. Display educational next steps and direct them to an appropriate suitability process.

Who receives an invitation?

Candidate trigger examples (proposed, not predictive investment advice):

  • Completed Style Finder and requested expert help or a meetup.
  • Viewed multiple basket factsheets and actively opted in for an event invitation.
  • Requested an explanation of investment modes, strategy, rebalancing or charges.
  • RM identifies a client who expressly wishes to attend, subject to contact permissions.

Do not invite solely because someone received an email or selected “aggressive” as a questionnaire response.

Invitation journey

  1. Tag interested customer and associate their home branch/region.
  2. Offer optional small-group product discovery session; disclose topic, location, time and purpose.
  3. Collect affirmative RSVP; provide easy decline and cancellation.
  4. Once 10–15 confirm, send logistics and appropriate reminders.
  5. Record attendance, questions, feedback and any requested assistance.
  6. Follow up with product information and optional action, not pressure.

Creative direction & production guidance

Visual language: warm ivory, fine detail, tactile objects, deliberate composition, deep green accents, editorial typography. Avoid stock-chart clichés, performance promises and hard-sell treatments. The idea should be clear in a glance.

Channel rules: email hero uses the full analogy and supporting promise; push stays concise with a single thought and tap target; in-app keeps the message scannable and links to Smartfolios exploration. Always include accessible alt text and safe cropping for mobile.

Approval note: these are design previews, not final deployment files. Verify live basket count, product terms, mandatory risk disclosures and legal entities with Product/Compliance before publishing. The visual approach does not imply assured returns.

07 / Measurement & planning

Interactive funnel and impact model

Change the planning assumptions to model digital and meetup demand. These values are entirely illustrative—not estimates, performance predictions or conversion targets.

This simplified illustrative pathway excludes some direct visitors who skip Style Finder. Meetup-interest leads are a subset of basket evaluators, not incremental prospects; they must not be added to digital totals. Compare actual observed cohorts with separately defined denominators.

Primary KPIs

  • Incremental first Smartfolios investors (versus randomised holdout)
  • Product visits and authenticated basket evaluations
  • Style Finder start-to-completion rate
  • Basket-detail views per qualified visitor
  • Meetup invitation → RSVP → attendance
  • Attendee post-session actions and assistance requests
  • First investments within an agreed attribution window

Recommended experiment design

Control: Standard awareness → Smartfolios product experience.

Variant A: Awareness → Style Finder → Smartfolios.

Variant B: Style Finder + optional local meetup invitation for appropriate high-intent customers.

Use customer-level random assignment where feasible, with branch capacity constraints considered in the meetup test. Separate impact on engagement from incremental investment; avoid counting self-selected attendees as proof of meetup effectiveness.
